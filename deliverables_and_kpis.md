# Deliverables and KPIs — Spark Nation Hackathon

## M - Media
- Deliverables: Poster designs (digital and printable), social media calendar, press release, asset repository.
- KPIs: Combined reach 15,000+, 500+ link clicks, at least 3 external mentions.

## A - Audience
- Deliverables: Registration form, outreach emails, community channels (Discord/WhatsApp), participant guide.
- KPIs: 200+ registrations, ≥80% attendance rate, ≥70% active engagement during the event.

## F - Financials
- Deliverables: Budget spreadsheet, sponsorship proposal deck, sponsor contact tracker, receipts ledger.
- KPIs: ₹50,000+ in sponsorship commitments, budget variance ≤5%.

## I - Infrastructure
- Deliverables: Venue confirmation, equipment list, network and power plan, refreshments plan, swag logistics.
- KPIs: Setup complete by D-1, zero critical equipment failures, ≥90% participant comfort rating.

## A - Administration
- Deliverables: Event timeline, volunteer roster and assignments, permission letters and approvals, incident response plan, feedback form.
- KPIs: Event start delay ≤10 minutes, ≥95% tasks completed on schedule, ≥90% positive feedback in post-event survey.
