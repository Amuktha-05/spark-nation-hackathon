# Media Plan

Purpose
-------
To define a clear communications and publicity plan that maximizes visibility and drives participant registrations for Spark Nation.

Target channels
---------------
- Instagram and LinkedIn (primary social platforms)  
- Campus mailing lists and notice boards  
- Student and alumni networks  
- Local tech communities and partner organizations

Activities and schedule
-----------------------
- Prepare poster and digital visual assets (two sizes: web and print).  
- Draft press release and sponsor acknowledgment templates.  
- Prepare a social media calendar with daily/weekly posts leading up to the event.  
- Produce short promotional videos or reels where possible.

Assets and repository
---------------------
Store all media assets under `/assets/media/` with clear naming conventions and version control.

Metrics
-------
- Reach, impressions and clicks per channel.  
- Conversion rate from view to registration.  
- Number of external media mentions.

Roles and responsibilities
--------------------------
- Media Lead: strategy, calendar and approvals.  
- Designer: asset creation and versions.  
- PR Coordinator: press outreach and partner liaison.
