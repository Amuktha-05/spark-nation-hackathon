# M - Media Plan

## 🎯 Objective
Promote Spark Nation Hackathon effectively through digital and offline channels to maximize visibility and participation.

## 🧩 Strategy
- Launch social media campaign (Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups)
- Coordinate press releases and poster designs
- Collaborate with campus media and local tech communities

## 🛠️ Deliverables
- Poster design set (digital + print)
- Event promo videos / reels
- Press release draft
- Social media calendar

## 📊 KPIs
| Metric | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Social media reach | 15,000+ |
| Engagement rate | >10% |
| Media coverage | 3+ mentions |

## 👥 Team Members
- Media Lead
